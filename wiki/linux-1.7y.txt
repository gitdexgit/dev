THE UNIX RITE OF PASSAGE

Stage 1: Months 0–6 (The Illusion of Control)
  "Look at this terminal. Look at the power. I can control everything."

Stage 2: Years 1–2 (The Tinkerer's Trap)
  "Look at my custom window manager, my 40 CLI tools, my shell plugins,
   and my status bar showing the weather in Tokyo. I spent all Saturday
   fixing a font glitch instead of writing code, but I feel like a god."

Stage 3: Year 3+ (The Enlightenment)
  "Get this garbage off my screen.
   Give me default bash, vi, standard tools, a scrap of paper,
   and let me get to work."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule: If you spend more time configuring your tools than building
      things with them, you are not working—you are playing with toys.

*Reached Stage 3 at 1.7y of Linux.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUT:
  Tinkering is how you actually learn the machine.

THE SOLUTION: Two users. Hard logout switch.

  • dex (Dexterity)
    The lab. Tinker, break things, build real skill.
    Rule: Augment only—never destroy baseline muscle memory.

  • imagine ("Imagine a world with zero config")
    The workspace. Pure vanilla defaults. No distractions.
    It just works—on any machine, anywhere on Earth.
