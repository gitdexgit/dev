# regulation over focus

THE REALITY
  You don't lack focus; you lack dopamine regulation.
  Cheap stimulation causes fake paralysis. Biology fixes it.

THE PROTOCOL
  1. Biology: Lift heavy, run, morning sun, eat liver and real food.
  2. Spirit:  The Quran brings sakinah. This dunya is just a means.
  3. Tools:   Vanilla Linux. No ricing. One scrap of paper on the desk.
  4. Wisdom:  Know the "why" behind your tools, the kids, and your impulses.

THE TRUTH
  Focus isn't forced with willpower.
  It is the byproduct of a tired body, a clean diet, and a quiet mind.
