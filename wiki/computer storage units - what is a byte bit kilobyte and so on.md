
Here is the binary storage units and such.

+----------+------+----------+
| Name     | Unit | Value    |
+----------+------+----------+
| Bit      | b    | 1 / 0    |
| Byte     | B    | 8 b      |
+----------+------+----------+
| Kilobyte | KB   | 1000 B   |
| Kibibyte | KiB  | 1024 B   |
+----------+------+----------+
| Megabyte | MB   | 1000 KB  |
| Mebibyte | MiB  | 1024 KiB |
+----------+------+----------+
| Gigabyte | GB   | 1000 MB  |
| Gibibyte | GiB  | 1024 MiB |
+----------+------+----------+
| Terabyte | TB   | 1000 GB  |
| Tibibyte | TiB  | 1024 GiB |
+----------+------+----------+
| Petabyte | PB   | 1000 TB  |
| Pebibyte | PiB  | 1024 TiB |
+----------+------+----------+
| Exabyte  | EB   | 1000 PB  |
| Exbibyte | EiB  | 1024 PiB |
+----------+------+----------+
