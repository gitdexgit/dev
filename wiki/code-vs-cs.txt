RULE: Anyone can write code that works. Computer science is solving problems using the least resources.

1. Linear search
   One step at a time. Correct, but slow. 1,000 items take up to 1,000 checks.

2. Binary search
   Cut the problem in half on every move. 1,000 items take at most 10 checks.

3. The metric
   Good design is not just finishing the task, but using the least CPU, RAM, and time.
