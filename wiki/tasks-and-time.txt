# execution over accounting

CORE PRINCIPLE
  Track output, not time. Tool maintenance is overhead.

THE 3 TIERS
  1. Active (Tape): ONE paper slip taped to bezel. You cannot pkill tape.
  2. Disk (Text):   ~/todo.txt + git commits. If cat can't read it, trash it.
  3. Wall (Slips):  "Not now" ideas. Walk past ambiently; rip down junk.

THE RULES
  1. 2-task ceiling:  1 active taped, max 1 parked on desk. Never a 3rd.
  2. Zero rollover:   Unfinished slips trash tonight. Brain remembers tomorrow.
  3. The 12:00 watch: Reset an analog watch to 12:00 for 1h blocks. No software.
  4. Pocket mobile:   Peel active slip into pocket + 1 blank card on the move.
  5. Real life wins:  Never duplicate lists. Water, family, toilet happen without guilt.

THE GOAL
  100% focus on the problem. Zero seconds on tool syntax.
