# the acoustic shield

CORE PRINCIPLE
  Familiar music on repeat is a shield, not entertainment.
  Effectiveness beats theoretical purity.

THE MECHANISM
  1. Zero surprise: Known tracks (Eroka) block noise with zero mental tax.
  2. Context bridge: Keep earbuds in during breaks to preserve the trance.

THE RULES
  1. Loop one track: No shuffling or song hunting during work.
  2. Known only:     Repetition is for focus; discovery is for leisure.
  3. Forget silence: If real life is noisy, an acoustic bubble wins.

THE GOAL
  Protect the focus bubble. Keep the mental thread unbroken.
