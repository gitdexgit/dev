GOAL: Learn C and pentesting together by writing simple C programs and trying to break them.

1. Study C99 slowly
   Read K. N. King, take notes on paper, and write small test programs without rushing.

2. Break your own code
   Reproduce classic bugs on purpose, starting with scanf %s buffer overflows, to see how vulnerabilities happen in memory.

3. Test compiler flags
   Compile with -Wall -Wextra first, then compare normal builds against hardened flags to see what warnings catch and what slips through.
