TMUX-CLIPBOARD(1)

NAME
    tmux-clipboard — copy tmux’s current buffer to the system clipboard

TIP
    Prefer your terminal’s mouse selection and copy/paste. Keep tmux vanilla.

    If you need text copied inside tmux and its buffer isn’t synced to the
    system clipboard, run:

        tmux show-buffer | xclip -selection clipboard

NOTE
    This copies tmux’s current buffer. Copy the text in tmux first.
