USER: DEX
"To be dexterous, you must tinker. The lab where tools are forged."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE IDENTITY
  imagine = The baseline. Zero config, pure survival.
  dex     = The laboratory. Experiment, automate, sharpen the blade.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RULES
  1. Augment, Never Destroy:
     Add power on top of defaults (<leader> keys, LSPs, fuzzy finders).
     Never overwrite core verbs (y, d, p, :w, POSIX pipes). 

  2. The Maintenance Audit:
     If a tweak saves 2 seconds but costs 2 hours to debug—kill it.
     Keep high-ROI tools; discard brittle micro-hacks without regret.

  3. Research & Development:
     Explore new paradigms (vis, structural regex, custom shell scripts).
     Test freely in ~/.local/bin—this is the sandbox.

  4. Distill for imagine:
     Every useful script must be translated into a raw, plain-text
     recipe in the wiki. dex invents; imagine needs the instructions.

  5. Commit and Sync:
     Push dotfiles and wiki to GitHub. What is learned in dex must
     be available to pull from anywhere on Earth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CYCLE
  1. Break and build in dex.
  2. Document the raw commands in the wiki.
  3. Git push.
  4. Log out. Back to imagine.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule: Play with toys to learn the machine—never to hide from the work.
