# taming hyper-focus

THE REALITY
  Hyper-focus is mental superglue.
  Whatever you touch first, your brain gets glued to.
  You don't lack focus; you lack an easy release.

THE TRAP
  Losing 6 hours to configs and rabbit holes while real work starves.

THE RULES
  1. Paper first:  Write ONE target before touching the keyboard.
  2. Hard stops:   Use prayer, the gym, or an alarm. Willpower fails.
  3. Starve ricing: Polishing tools is fake work to avoid hard work.

THE GOAL
  Point the engine at CCNA and code, never configuration.
