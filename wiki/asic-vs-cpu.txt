RULE: Be the workshop, not the stamping machine.

1. The factory (ASIC)
   Built to do one job over and over. Fast, but obsolete the second the requirements change.

2. The workshop (CPU)
   Uses basic tools, reads any new blueprint, and adapts on the fly. Slower, but impossible to replace.

3. The survival test
   If your workday is a fixed checklist, a script will take it. If you can fix any broken system using bare tools, you stay
   dangerous anywhere.
